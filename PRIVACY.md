# Privacy Policy

  **Last updated: July 14, 2026**

  ## Overview

  Simple Count is a countdown widget app for iOS. Your privacy is
  important to us. This policy explains what data Simple Count does
  and does not collect.

  ## Data Collection

  Simple Count does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal
  data. All information you enter — event names, dates, notes,
  photos, and settings — is stored **locally on your device** and,
  if you are signed in to iCloud, in **your own personal iCloud**
  (see iCloud Backup below). We cannot access either.

  The only data that ever becomes visible to anyone else is what you
  explicitly share with the optional **Send to a Friend** feature
  described below.

  ## What Is Stored Locally

  - Event names, dates, and notes
  - Event photos (stored on your device)
  - App settings and preferences

  ## iCloud Backup

  Simple Count can back up your events, notes, and photos to **your
  personal iCloud account** so they survive if you delete and
  reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone. Backup is on by default
  and works only while you are signed in to iCloud on your device.

  - The backup is stored in your **private iCloud database** on
  Apple's servers, under Apple's iCloud terms of service.
  - **We cannot access, read, or receive this data.** Apple does not
  give app developers access to users' private iCloud databases.
  - The backup uses your iCloud storage, not our servers — Simple
  Count has no servers.
  - You can turn backup off — or delete the backup from your iCloud
  entirely — at any time in **Settings → iCloud Backup** inside the
  app.

  ## Optional Sharing Feature (Send to a Friend)

  Simple Count includes an optional feature that lets you share
  events with other people. When you use this feature:

  - The selected event data (name, date, emoji, note, and optionally
  a photo) is uploaded to **Apple's iCloud (CloudKit)**
  infrastructure.
  - A unique share link is generated. Anyone with this link can
  import the event into their own copy of Simple Count.
  - Shared data is automatically **deleted after 7 days**.
  - You control when and what you share. This feature is never
  triggered automatically.

  This data is stored on Apple's servers under Apple's iCloud terms
  of service. Simple Count does not have its own servers and does
  not access or log shared data beyond what Apple's CloudKit
  provides.

  ## Third Parties

  Simple Count does not use any third-party analytics, advertising
  SDKs, or tracking tools.

  The iCloud Backup and sharing features use **Apple CloudKit**
  (Apple's own cloud infrastructure). No data is shared with any
  other third party.

  ## Notifications

  If you enable day-of notifications, these are handled entirely by
  iOS on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

  ## Children's Privacy

  Simple Count does not collect data from anyone, including children
  under 13.
